01 / RESEARCH

Turning observations into physical insight.

My current research centers on nearby dwarf galaxies and astronomical data pipelines, from image preprocessing to interpretable physical models.

CURRENT · SUT

Two-component dust emission in dwarf galaxies

Developing a multi-band SED pipeline for LITTLE THINGS galaxies, including resolution matching, uncertainty estimation, and Bayesian/MCMC modified-blackbody fitting.

INO · DETECTION

Stellar-source detection with wavelets

Built an automated method using dyadic wavelet transforms to enhance point sources and suppress background structure in crowded, low-S/N images.

INTERNSHIP · IPM

Exposure-time tools for observatory planning

Contributed to an ESO-inspired Phase II environment for INO and extended its calculator to model S/N, saturation, and saturated sources in the field of view.
